    Why this matters for scanned paperwork

    The most common reason to turn photos into a PDF is paperwork: you photograph a few pages of a form, an ID document or a stack of receipts and need one file to send. Those are among the most sensitive things you own, and handing them to a free conversion site means they sit on a stranger's server. Here the PDF is assembled in your browser, so the photos are never transmitted.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The PDF is built inside your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

    How many photos can I include?

    There is no fixed limit, but since this uses your device's memory, a few dozen at a time is more comfortable.

    Will my photos be cropped?

    No. Each image is scaled down to fit inside the page while keeping its original proportions.

    Is a watermark added?

    No watermark, and no usage limit.
