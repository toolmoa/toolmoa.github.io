Static codes, not tracked links

Many QR generators quietly produce a dynamic code: the image points at their own short link, which then redirects to your address. That lets them count every scan — and it means your code stops working the day they shut the service down or move it behind a paywall. The codes here are static: your address is encoded directly into the pattern, nothing sits in between, and the code keeps working forever regardless of what happens to this site.

Frequently asked questions

Will my QR code expire?

No. The content is encoded into the image itself, so there is no server in the middle and nothing to expire.

Which error correction level should I pick?

M is fine for most uses. Choose H if you plan to place a logo over the centre, so the code still scans when partly covered.

My code will not scan.

Usually the contrast is too low or the quiet zone is missing. Keep the margin at Normal, the code dark and the background light. Inverted codes (light code on dark background) fail on many phones.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. No watermark, no attribution required, no usage limit.

What size should I use for print?

Save at 1024px or larger and print it at least 2cm (about 0.8in) wide.
