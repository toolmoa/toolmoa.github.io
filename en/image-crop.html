Cropping does not always remove what you cut

Some editors mark a region as hidden rather than actually discarding it, which is how cropped screenshots have leaked the parts people thought they had removed. This tool redraws only the selected pixels onto a new canvas and saves that — the discarded area is genuinely gone from the output file, and the original photo is never uploaded in the first place.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping and rotation happen inside your browser, and nothing is transmitted.

Does cropping reduce quality?

Save as PNG and there is no quality loss at all. The crop is taken from the full-resolution original, so the result keeps the original detail even though the preview is displayed smaller.

How do I make a square profile picture?

Choose 1:1 under "Lock aspect ratio" and the selection stays square however you drag it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Drag the selection with your finger, or grab a corner handle to resize it.

Can I crop several images at once?

This tool handles one at a time. To shrink many files at once, use Compress image.
