    Why merge PDFs offline

    The documents people most often need to merge — signed contracts, scanned invoices, expense receipts, medical paperwork — are exactly the documents that should not be uploaded to a service you have never heard of. Most free PDF sites process your file on their servers and keep it there for some period of time. This page loads a PDF library into your browser and does the whole job locally, so there is no upload, no queue and no retention policy to read.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDFs really not uploaded?

    Correct. You can verify it: load this page, turn off your internet connection, and the merge still works. Nothing is transmitted at any point.

    How many files can I merge, and how large?

    There is no imposed limit. The practical ceiling is your device's memory, so several hundred megabytes may become slow on an older machine.

    Can it open password-protected PDFs?

    No. Encrypted PDFs cannot be opened. Remove the password first, then merge.

    How do I change the order?

    Drag an item in the list, or use the ↑ and ↓ buttons on each row. Files are joined from top to bottom.

    Are bookmarks and links preserved?

    Page content and links inside pages are preserved. The document-level outline (bookmarks) may be dropped during the merge.
